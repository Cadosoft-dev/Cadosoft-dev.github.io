# Hi, we're Cadosoft

Cadosoft is an indie app studio building Android apps and mobile games. We publish 10 apps and games on Google Play, including an AI photo enhancer (Upscalia), a cartoon photo app (Totoon) and a handful of casual games. We keep our apps light and focused, and as a small independent team we ship updates quickly and welcome feedback.

## What we make
- AI photo tools for Android that sharpen and restyle pictures
- Simple everyday utilities
- Casual mobile games
